Frequently Asked Questions

Why does Jemixo Safe ask to scan installed apps?

Jemixo Safe uses the QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES permission exclusively to evaluate installed apps against known threat vectors (counterfeit banking APKs, malicious loan apps, and suspicious permission combinations). All analysis happens 100% locally on your device — no app list is ever uploaded to any server.

How do I switch the language to Hindi?

Tap the Settings icon in the app, then tap on Language (भाषा) and select हिंदी (Hindi). The entire interface, warning cards, and scan scores will immediately translate into clear, straightforward Hindi.

Is my personal WhatsApp chat data accessed?

Never. Jemixo Safe only inspects cached media folders (forwarded videos, duplicate pictures, and voice notes) to help you free up valuable device storage. It does not read your private chats, text messages, or encryption keys.
