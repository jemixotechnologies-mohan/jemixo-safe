Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 29, 2026 • Version: 1.1.0 • Application: Jemixo Safe (com.jemixo.safe)

Our Core Privacy Promise: Jemixo Safe operates on a strict 100% on-device architecture. The application has no user accounts, no tracking analytics, and no remote servers collecting personal data. All scans, file inspections, and risk indicators run entirely within your device CPU.

1. Information We Collect

We collect nothing. Jemixo Safe does not transmit, upload, sell, or share any personal information, device identifiers, contact lists, photos, videos, messages, or location data with any third party or remote server.

2. Data Stored on Your Phone

To provide device security diagnostics and storage cleanup history, the application maintains records locally on your device in an encrypted private SQLite database (in the app sandbox):

  • Scan Records & Score History: Timestamps and aggregate risk scores derived from past safety scans so you can compare changes over time.
  • User Preferences: Theme selection (Light/Dark/System), language selection (English/Hindi), simple mode toggle, and notification alerts toggle.

All local data can be erased at any time via the in-app Settings → Your data → Delete all button, or automatically by uninstalling the application from your phone.

3. Android Permissions & How They Are Used

Jemixo Safe requests only the minimum permissions necessary to perform its safety and cleanup functions:

A. Installed Apps Visibility (Package Intent Filtering)

Purpose: Device security and fraud prevention. To identify counterfeit banking apps, fake government portals, unverified high-interest loan apps, and apps requesting dangerous combinations of accessibility, SMS, and overlay permissions.

Privacy Guarantee: Installed launchable packages are inspected strictly on the device using local signature rules and threat databases without broad restricted queries. App details are never transmitted or uploaded over the internet.

B. Photos, Videos, and Media (READ_MEDIA_IMAGES, READ_MEDIA_VIDEO, READ_MEDIA_AUDIO)

Purpose: Storage analysis and WhatsApp cleaner. Used only when you enter the "Clean" tab to identify duplicate files, old forwarded WhatsApp videos, voice notes, and large files.

Privacy Guarantee: Thumbnails and file hashes are calculated entirely locally in memory. Files are only deleted after you review them and explicitly tap the confirm button.

C. Camera (CAMERA)

Purpose: QR Code safety scanner. The camera stream is activated strictly while the QR scanner screen is open to read QR codes and reveal embedded UPI payment recipients or website links before you tap.

Privacy Guarantee: No camera images or video feeds are ever recorded, saved, or uploaded.

D. Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS)

Purpose: Optional new app install warnings. If enabled in Settings, the background worker checks every 30 minutes for newly sideloaded apps that request sensitive permissions.

4. Scam Data & Rule Updates

Once weekly, the application queries a static, publicly hosted JSON file to download updated scam phrases, verified official bank package names, and cybercrime patterns. This HTTP GET request carries no device identifiers, no user tokens, and no telemetry.

5. Sharing & User-Initiated Actions

When you choose to share a security summary card or export a PDF report, the document is generated locally on your phone and handed over to Android's native system share sheet. You decide which contact or application receives it. Jemixo Safe never intercepts or logs this sharing action.

6. Children’s Privacy

Jemixo Safe does not knowingly collect any data from children under the age of 13. The application is a general diagnostic and storage utility safe for all age groups.

7. Compliance with Indian DPDP Act 2023 & Global Standards

In accordance with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act), the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and Google Play Developer Program Policies:

  • Principle of Data Minimization: Zero data collection principle.
  • Right to Erasure: One-tap complete wiping of all on-device historical data.
  • No Third-Party SDKs: No tracking SDKs, no ad networks (AdMob, Unity, etc.), and no analytics engines (Firebase, Mixpanel, etc.).
