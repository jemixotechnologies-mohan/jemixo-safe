Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear and transparent answers about how Jemixo Safe protects your device.

Does Jemixo Safe send my photos, files or contacts to any server? ▼
No, absolutely never. All scans, hashes, and media checks happen directly on your device CPU. Jemixo Safe has no server backend for user data, no analytics trackers, and no ad networks.
What is "Digital Arrest" and how does Jemixo Safe help? ▼
"Digital Arrest" is a dangerous cyber fraud where criminals impersonate police, CBI, customs, or court officials over WhatsApp video calls and threaten victims with fake arrest warrants. In Indian law, no agency arrests or fines people over video calls. Jemixo Safe provides immediate step-by-step guidance on how to hang up safely and provides one-tap dialing to the Government of India's 1930 Cyber Crime Helpline.
Will WhatsApp Cleaner delete my important family pictures? ▼
No. The cleaner selectively targets received forwards, voice notes, stickers, and exact duplicates. You have full control with preview thumbnails and multi-select checkboxes. Nothing is deleted without your explicit confirmation.
Is the app available in Hindi? ▼
Yes! Jemixo Safe has complete, authentic Hindi (हिन्दी) support across every single screen, navigation tab, scanner, and report. You can easily switch between English and Hindi with one tap in Settings.
How do I delete my data from Jemixo Safe? ▼
Because all data is stored exclusively in your phone's internal storage, you can go to Settings → Your data → Delete all. Alternatively, uninstalling the app from Android completely and irreversibly removes all stored records. Learn more on our Data Deletion Policy page.
